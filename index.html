Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Answers

Do you make custom pillows and cushions?

Yes. We make custom pillows, lumbar pillows, indoor cushions, outdoor cushions, and soft furnishings based on your fabric, size, and style needs.

Do you work on bedding and table linens?

Yes. We work on bedspreads, duvet covers, bolsters, decorative shams, tablecloths, runners, and other custom home decor sewing projects.

Do you make Roman shades?

Yes. We can create Roman shades and fabric window treatments. Please share your measurements, fabric details, and style preference for a quote.

Do you work on Indian traditional wear?

Yes. We offer Indian traditional repairs and alterations, including salwar kameez alterations, blouse fitting, saree pico stitching, and saree fall stitching.

Do you offer rush or same-day alterations?

Same-day alterations may be available depending on the type of repair, fabric, and current workload. Please contact us with your item details and timeline so we can confirm availability.
